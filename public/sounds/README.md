# Sound Files

## Required Files

1. **ringtone.mp3** - Call ringtone (looping)
   - Place your ringtone file here
   - Recommended: 5-10 seconds, loopable
   - Format: MP3, 128kbps

2. **notification.mp3** - Toast notification sound
   - Place your notification sound here
   - Recommended: 1-2 seconds, short and pleasant
   - Format: MP3, 128kbps

## Where to Get Sounds

Free sound resources:
- https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/notification/
- https://freesound.org/
- https://www.zapsplat.com/

## How to Add

1. Download/create your sound files
2. Rename them to:
   - `ringtone.mp3`
   - `notification.mp3`
3. Place them in this `/public/sounds/` directory
4. The app will automatically use them
